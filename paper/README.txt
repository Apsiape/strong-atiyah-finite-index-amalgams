The strong Atiyah conjecture and von Neumann ranks for graphs of groups with finite-index edge inclusions
Nidhal Mghirbi and Seth Douglas

Build: python -I -B build.py --build-dir NEW_OR_EMPTY_DIRECTORY
Requires Python 3 and pdflatex. The bibliography is inline; no figures or
external files are needed. The build runs pdflatex three times without shell
escape and rejects warnings, unresolved references and overfull or underfull
boxes. main.pdf is the compiled preprint (21 pages).
